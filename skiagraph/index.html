그림자가 해의 시각을 가리킵니다

지금 해와 달이 있는 방향에서 온 빛이 영침의 그림자를 그릇 안에 드리웁니다. 그림자 끝은 실제 해시계처럼 진태양시와 절기를 가리킵니다.

선은 내 위도로 긋습니다

그릇 안의 시각선과 절기선을 내 위치의 위도로 계산합니다. 서울의 앙부일구와 런던의 앙부일구는 모양이 다릅니다.

시계로 바로 읽힙니다

A sundial that reads the sun's time in shadow, and clock time in numbers

The angbuilgu, the hemispherical bowl sundial first made in Korea in 1434, drawn in 3D for the place where you are.

Coming to the App Store

Your own angbuilgu

The shadow shows the sun's time

Light from where the sun and moon are right now casts the needle's shadow into the bowl. Its tip shows true solar time and the season, as on a real sundial.

Lines drawn for your latitude

The hour lines and season lines inside the bowl are computed for where you are. A bowl for Seoul and a bowl for London look different.

It reads as a clock

The current time glows on the rim. The app also explains, with the numbers for right now, why sundial time and clock time differ.

A clock to leave on

  • Dock Mode keeps the screen on and shows the time large. On iPhone and iPad it starts by itself when the device stands in landscape while charging.
  • At night the bowl is lit by moonlight when a bright moon is up, and by a lamp when it is not. In Real Sun mode the shadow goes when the sun sets.
  • Four materials, all free: bronze with silver inlay, patinated bronze, brass and black stone.
  • The rim is engraved in Hangul with the names of the double-hours and the 24 solar terms. The app explains how to read them and what they mean.
  • VoiceOver reads the time and where the shadow is pointing.

Skiagraph Pro

  • No ads
  • Studio mode
  • Astronomy panel — sunrise and sunset, the sun's position, what the shadow points to

A one-time purchase. The free version shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen on iPhone and iPad; the ad is hidden in Dock Mode.

Good to know

The glowing numbers and the night lamp are additions; a real angbuilgu has neither. The app's About screen lists where it departs from a real sundial. The positions of the sun and moon are computed on your device, and no account is needed.
